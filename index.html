About

the layer everything else sits on

I split my attention between rock and proof. On one side, mining engineering: the physical, high-stakes discipline of extracting resources responsibly. On the other, pure mathematics, which I study for its own sake — I hold a Platonist view of it, the belief that mathematics is discovered rather than invented, and I keep coming back to Euler and Ramanujan as proof of what that discovery can look like.

Outside the classroom, I dance, and I teach. I work as a part-time substitute teacher at my former high school, KwaMahlobo Secondary School, where I was Class of 2024's top achiever.also served as School President at Esiyalwini Junior Secondary School. I also represent UJ internationally as its Women in Mining representative.

My plan isn't a straight line, and I'd rather be upfront about that than pretend otherwise: finish my engineering degree, work in the industry for long enough to build real experience and savings, and then pursue a Master's in Mathematics abroad.
